# LuckySparrow Softwarehouse Operating Model

Stage 0 is configuration only. Codex in the setup chat configures Paperclip; Paperclip agents remain idle unless the board explicitly starts Stage 1.

Stage 1 begins only after board approval. Agents then work first on Soar and Roost. Other apps remain parked unless the board activates them.

Stage 2 moves Paperclip to VPS and operates with Roost as an autonomous digital-services company.

Paperclip is the control plane. Product apps stay in their own repositories. Work must stay company-scoped, issue-scoped, evidence-backed, and reversible where possible.
