# Innovation To Product Lifecycle

Department 11 Innovation is an incubator, not the permanent home for every app.

## Rule

Keep app projects in 11 Innovation while the company is discovering, validating, architecting, and proving the product. When an app is usable, supportable, deployable, and commercially meaningful, propose a governed transition to 02 Product.

Examples:

- incubation: 11 Innovation: Soar
- accepted product/service phase: 02 Product: Soar

Do not rename or move a project only because it has code, a repo, or a deployed URL. The product/service department means the app can be used, maintained, packaged, sold, or granted access with appropriate evidence.

## Transition Packet

Before proposing a move from 11 Innovation to 02 Product, provide:

- product name and repo;
- architecture source of truth;
- core user outcomes proven;
- local and production readiness evidence;
- supportability and monitoring evidence;
- pricing, access, entitlement, or packaging assumptions if commercial;
- documentation and onboarding state;
- known risks and blockers;
- owner decision requested: move now, keep incubating, or stop/park.

## Department Handoff

After the product transition:

- 02 Product owns product/service refinement, roadmap, packaging, access, and onboarding.
- 03 Sales joins for offer, pricing, messaging, and conversion paths.
- 04 Operations and 09 Technology keep delivery, deployment, monitoring, and quality healthy.
- 05 Customer Success owns support, feedback, user success, and improvement input.
- 07 Finance and 10 Legal join for cost, payments, compliance, privacy, and risk.
