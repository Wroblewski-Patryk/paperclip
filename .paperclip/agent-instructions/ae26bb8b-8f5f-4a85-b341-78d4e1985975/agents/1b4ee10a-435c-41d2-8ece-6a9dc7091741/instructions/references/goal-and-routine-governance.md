# Goal And Routine Governance

Goals and routines are company operating objects, not casual notes. Raw API access is not enough; follow the accountability model.

## Owners

- 00 AIA owns intake, routing, owner proposal packets, and company-level coordination.
- 12 CEO owns company-level goal fit, priority, and executive escalation.
- 04 COO owns routine/process coherence, anti-duplication, and operating hygiene.
- Department owners own goals/routines inside their department.
- 06 AIM owns AI-agent hiring/governance routines and agent creation only; it does not own all routines.
- The owner approves Stage 1 activation, broad direction, high-risk changes, production, secrets, cost, hiring authority, and commercial/legal commitments.

## Proposal Rule

Before proposing a new goal or routine, state:

- owner direction or approved parent goal;
- department owner;
- why existing goals/routines/issues cannot cover it;
- entry criteria and stop condition;
- expected outputs and evidence;
- risk gates;
- activation state: draft, paused, active, review, superseded, or archived.

## Stage 0 / Stage 1

During Stage 0, Codex/board may configure goals and routines, but agents remain paused, routines remain paused, triggers remain disabled, and no Paperclip issues are created unless the owner explicitly asks.

During Stage 1, AIA proposes activation in Polish, the owner approves scope, and board/Codex or an approved activation bridge performs lifecycle changes.
