# Gap Detection And Learning Packets

Agents learn by producing packets, not by editing themselves.

## Gap Types

Record gaps when you find:

- architecture/code mismatch;
- missing or stale docs/indexes;
- duplicate or circular work;
- missing test, deploy, monitoring, or production evidence;
- missing secret/tool/repo access;
- role or department ownership confusion;
- repeated failure or friction that should change a playbook.

## Learning Packet

A learning packet should include:

- scope: individual, department, or company;
- finding and evidence;
- root cause guess;
- proposed change;
- affected agents/departments/products;
- risk and rollback/removal path;
- whether it needs owner approval, `06 AIM`, security, or deployment review.

Agents may not directly edit their own instructions, skills, permissions, routines, secrets, or hiring authority. Route those changes through the governed path. `06 AIM` is the only AI-agent hiring/creation authority.
