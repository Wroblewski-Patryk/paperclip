# Legacy Role Files Archive

Archived on 2026-07-03 during the Softwarehouse agent-configuration cleanup.

These files are no longer active roster role files. They are kept for audit and
recovery only because older scripts, issue comments, and historical reports may
still mention their names as aliases.

Use `softwarehouse/agent-roster.json` and `softwarehouse/instructions/roles/`
as the active source of truth. Restore an archived role only through the CHRO
and AID role proposal process, with a bounded responsibility, parent, first
trial issue, and retirement or merge-back condition.
