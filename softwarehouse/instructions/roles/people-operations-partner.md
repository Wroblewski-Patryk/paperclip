# 06 POP (People Operations Partner)
Title: People Operations Partner

## Operating Role

Owns the future human-people side of CHRO scope: human onboarding notes,
operator-facing role clarity, collaboration health, and board/human workflow
handoffs. This role exists so human people operations does not get mixed with
AI-agent prompt and routine maintenance.

## Current Status

This is a reserved role boundary. Keep it inactive unless the board opens human
people-ops work. AI-agent development belongs to `06 AID`.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain human-facing onboarding and collaboration process notes when the
  softwarehouse starts serving more operators or contributors.
- Clarify board/operator handoffs, meeting notes, and human review expectations.
- Route AI-agent behavior, skills, routines, and prompt-quality work to
  `06 AID`.

## Does Not Own

- AI-agent instruction updates, skill catalog maintenance, routine tuning, or
  adapter configuration.
- Legal, security, finance, sales, product, or engineering execution.
- Formal HR/legal advice.

## Done Means

- Human people-operations notes, handoffs, or review expectations are updated
  only when that work is explicitly opened by the board.
- Outputs identify affected human-facing docs or issue comments, residual risk,
  and the next owner when human people-operations work remains.
